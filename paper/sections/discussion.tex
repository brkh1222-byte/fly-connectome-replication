The Shiu et al.\ model, run unchanged, reproduced its feeding and grooming predictions on the
connectome it was built from, and those predictions disappeared when the wiring was shuffled.
On the two other connectomes the outcome depended on synapse density. BANC, with about
0.45 times FlyWire's input per cell type, was silent at the readouts. The male CNS, with
about 1.76 times, reproduced the predictions only together with brain-wide activity. This
confirms, with a third connectome, the conclusion of earlier ports that the per-synapse
weight does not transfer between reconstructions \citep{santos2026,brainlab2026,fugsukjit2026}.

The degraded-FlyWire control separates two parts of the FlyWire--BANC gap. Removing synapses
from FlyWire until each cell type matched BANC's input abolished sugar$\to$MN9, so BANC's
failure on this prediction as released needs no biological explanation. But one global
rescaling brought the prediction back in degraded FlyWire and not in BANC, and BANC's
response profiles were further from FlyWire's than those of any degraded copy. Uniform
per-type synapse loss is therefore not sufficient. The remainder could come from
non-uniform synapse detection within cell types or brain regions \citep{santos2026}, from
different transmitter predictions, from differences in cell typing (for example, BANC has
60 neurons typed LB3b against 25 in FlyWire), or from real differences between the two
flies. Our control cannot distinguish these.

The male CNS did not agree with FlyWire better than BANC did, and no pair of connectomes
agreed as well as the two hemispheres of FlyWire or of the male CNS. Because each connectome
differs from the others in animal, laboratory, pipeline and (for the male) sex at once, this
ordering cannot be read as an effect of sex or individuality.

A practical observation is that the model has two regimes, sparse and brain-wide, and that
which one a network enters for a given stimulus is fragile: it changed with side, with
normalisation and between connectomes. Rank-agreement metrics then mostly measure whether
two networks are in the same regime. Reports of cross-connectome agreement should state the
fraction of active neurons alongside any correlation.

\textbf{Limitations.} Each connectome is one animal. Sex is confounded with laboratory and
reconstruction pipeline. Signs come from predicted transmitters, and Shiu's rule makes every
neuron that is not GABAergic or glutamatergic excitatory, including 14{,}365 male CNS neurons
with an ``unclear'' prediction. BANC's typed sensory neurons are unbalanced between sides
(water GRNs 3 vs 15; JO-F 37 vs 108), which weakens its hemisphere comparison. The degraded
control thins synapses uniformly within a postsynaptic cell type, which is only one model of
incompleteness. Two replication criteria were tightened after seeing the first tables
(Methods). The brain-only subgraphs omit ascending input. The model has no gap junctions or
neuromodulation. Water$\to$MN9 could not be tested at the common stimulation rate.
